# Logistics Performance Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview

This project analyses logistics operations using Microsoft Excel, focusing on delivery efficiency, carrier performance, warehouse utilisation, revenue generation and profitability.

I cleaned and standardised the dataset before developing an interactive dashboard to help identify operational patterns, compare logistics performance and highlight opportunities for improving business efficiency.

The dashboard combines financial and operational KPIs with interactive charts and slicers, allowing users to investigate different aspects of logistics performance.

##  Project Objectives

- Evaluate overall logistics revenue and profitability.
- Compare carrier performance based on delivery reliability and speed.
- Examine warehouse capacity and utilisation.
- Identify the most profitable and highest-revenue products.
- Analyse shipment outcomes across destination regions.
- Investigate monthly revenue, operational costs and profit trends.
- Provide practical recommendations for improving logistics efficiency.

## Dashboard Analysis and Key Findings

### 1. On Time Delivery Performance by Carrier

**Analysis:** Compared logistics carriers based on their contributions to on-time deliveries.

**Key Finding:** LocalExpress recorded the highest share of on-time deliveries at 18.39%. DHL and FedEx also demonstrated strong performance.

**Operational Insight:** LocalExpress is an important contributor to punctual deliveries. Carrier-level delivery rates should also be considered when evaluating reliability.

### 2. Average Delivery Days by Carrier

**Analysis:** Compared the average delivery time across different logistics providers.

**Key Finding:** FedEx recorded the shortest average delivery time, while DHL and UPS recorded the longest.

**Operational Insight:** FedEx may be a suitable option for time-sensitive shipments, particularly when faster delivery is a priority.

### 3. Delivery Status by Carrier

**Analysis:** Compared delivered, delayed, cancelled and in-transit shipments across carriers.

**Key Findings:**

- Successfully delivered shipments outnumbered delayed and cancelled shipments.
- LocalExpress handled the highest shipment volume.

**Operational Insight:** Shipment completion is substantial, but the overall 56% on-time delivery rate suggests that delivery punctuality requires further attention.

### 4. Available Warehouse Capacity

**Analysis:** Evaluated the remaining storage capacity across warehouses.

**Key Finding:** WH South recorded the highest available storage capacity.

**Operational Insight:** WH South could accommodate additional inventory, making it a potential location for future storage expansion.

### 5. Average Warehouse Utilisation Rate

**Analysis:** Compared the proportion of available storage capacity being used across warehouses.

**Key Finding:** WH East recorded the highest warehouse utilisation rate.

**Operational Insight:** WH East is operating closer to its storage capacity and may require monitoring to prevent congestion or operational bottlenecks.

### 6. Top Revenue-Generating Products

**Analysis:** Ranked products according to total revenue generated.

**Key Finding:** Beverage Packs generated the highest revenue.

**Business Insight:** Beverage Packs represent an important contributor to sales and could be prioritised when planning inventory and marketing activities.

### 7. Top Profit Generating Products

**Analysis:** Compared the profitability of different product categories.

**Key Finding:** Beverage Packs and Engine Oil generated the highest profits.

**Business Insight:** These products make important contributions to overall profitability and deserve attention in product allocation and inventory decisions.

### 8. Delivery Status by Destination Region

**Analysis:** Examined shipment outcomes across different geographical regions.

**Key Finding:** Delivered shipments represented the largest shipment-status category across regions.

**Operational Insight:** Regional comparisons provide a useful starting point for identifying differences in delivery performance and areas requiring additional attention.

### 9. Monthly Revenue, Cost and Profit Trends

**Analysis:** Tracked logistics revenue, operational costs and profits over time.

**Key Findings:**

- Revenue remained higher than operational costs throughout the period analysed.
- Monthly profitability remained positive.

**Business Insight:** The dataset shows positive financial performance during the observed period. Continued monitoring of operational costs will help protect profitability.

### 2. Optimise Carrier Selection

Consider FedEx for time sensitive deliveries based on its shorter average delivery time. Evaluate LocalExpress further for reliability focused shipments.

### 3. Monitor Warehouse Capacity

Regularly review utilisation levels at WH East to avoid potential storage constraints.

### 4. Improve Inventory Allocation

Consider allocating additional inventory to WH South, which has the highest available storage capacity.

### 5. Prioritise High Performing Products

Use the strong revenue and profit contributions of Beverage Packs and Engine Oil to guide inventory and marketing decisions.

### 6. Monitor Operational Costs and Profitability

Continue tracking monthly revenue, costs and profits to identify changes in financial performance.
